How Keepers Work

Checked against 101 picks

One number follows a player for as long as anyone holds him. It gets two rounds more expensive every season, and when it would cost better than a first-round pick, he goes back in the pool.

A waiver pickup's full life
RD 11→ RD 9→ RD 7→ RD 5→ RD 3→ RD 1 · last year→ Pool

Where it starts

Two rounds better than where he was drafted. A round 9 pick keeps at round 7. Waiver pickups start at round 11.

It climbs

Hold him another season and the cost drops two more rounds. No cap on years — the ladder runs out on its own.

When he's gone

Once the next cost lands past a first-rounder he isn't keepable. That's why rounds 1 and 2 are locked the moment they're drafted.

The number is his

Trade him or claim him off waivers and the cost comes along. Nobody resets a keeper clock by cycling a player.

Two per team

Each keeper takes that round's pick, so keeping at round 3 costs you your third-rounder.

Same round twice

If both keepers would land on the same round, the second one moves one more round up: −2 and −3. Two waiver pickups come back as your 11th and 10th. Voted Feb 2025, 9–0.

Locked before playoffs

The frozen roster decides eligibility. Drops during the playoffs don't cost you a keeper.

Due a week before the draft

Keepers are declared to the commissioner one week before draft day so everyone can plan. Yahoo's own keeper date is only when its tool wants them typed in.

The Roster Lock

Why the snapshot matters

Rosters lock before the playoffs, and that frozen roster — not whatever the team looks like in April — is what keeper eligibility is judged from. Yahoo doesn't preserve it, which is why it currently lives as screenshots pasted into a spreadsheet.

What gets frozen

All ten rosters exactly as they stand at the lock, bench and IL included.

What it decides

Who each manager may keep. Moves after the lock add and remove nothing.

Why it exists

Playoff teams churn rosters chasing a title. Without the lock, chasing a ring would cost you keepers.

What the site will do

One button, one timestamp, all ten rosters kept forever and still readable years later.

League Policies

Decided in the group chat, recorded here

Draft order

Reverse order of the regular-season standings — the worst record picks first, the best record picks last — regardless of what happened in the playoffs. Ties go to the head-to-head result. Snake from there.

Over the games cap

Caught the same day, the extra player comes out — your opponent picks which, or the commissioner pulls the best one. Not caught in time and it's an automatic loss; only Yahoo support can change the record after the fact.

Short weeks

The cap moves with the NBA schedule. NBA Cup weeks, election week and All-Star week get their own number, set by the commissioner and put to a vote when it's close.

Last place

Makes 100 free throws inside an hour, on one continuous video for the group. Voted Oct 2025.

The belt

The champion holds the championship belt for the year — first awarded for 2024-25 — and passes it on by mail or in person at the next draft.

Playoff churn

Playoff teams can add and drop freely; keeper eligibility was frozen at the roster lock, so nothing you do in the playoffs costs you a keeper.

Changing a rule

Rules change by a league vote before the season, not mid-year. Anything else waits for the offseason — put it on the list.
